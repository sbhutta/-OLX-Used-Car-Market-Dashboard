# 🚘 OLX Car Dashboard  
An interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing used car listings from **OLX Pakistan**, delivering insights into market trends, pricing dynamics, brand popularity, and city-level variations. Designed for automotive dealers, buyers, and analysts to make data-driven decisions and benchmark car values.  

---

## 📋 Project Overview  
The **OLX Car Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of Pakistan’s used car market. It reveals patterns in listings, pricing, and car attributes, allowing users to filter results by brand, model, city, fuel type, year, and transmission.  

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack  
- 📊 **Power BI** – Data modeling, visualization, and KPI tracking  
- 🧮 **DAX** – Custom measures (average price, total listings, mileage impact)  
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation  
- 📄 **Microsoft Excel** – Preprocessing and manual data adjustments  
- 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` (Power BI Report), `.png` (Preview Images)  

---

## 🔍 Data Source  
**Source:** OLX Pakistan Used Car Listings (public dataset, cleaned manually)  

Includes:  
- Brand, model, year, city, fuel type, transmission, and assembly  
- Price and mileage data  
- Historical listings across multiple years  

---

## ✨ Features / Highlights  

**Business Problem**  
Car buyers, sellers, and dealerships lack quick, aggregated insights into Pakistan’s used car market. Manual browsing makes it difficult to compare models, assess fair pricing, and spot market shifts.  

**Goal**  
Build an interactive dashboard to explore pricing trends, brand popularity, mileage impact, and city-wise market activity—streamlining decision-making for buyers, sellers, and analysts.
